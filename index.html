About Me

Hi! I am a CS Master’s student at ETH Zürich. I'm interested in formal methods, semantics, and concurrency.

In my free time, i am promoting free and open source software at TheAlt and organize the board game days.

Availability

I am currently not looking for a new project or thesis. However, if you are an exceptionally strong and motivated advisor with a proven background in Programming Languages and Verification, please feel free to reach out :)

Theses

  • Bachelor thesis - "Specifying and Verifying Page Tables in a Concurrent Operating System"

    In my Bachelor thesis, I dived into state machine modeling and proof work in Verus, when I explored how to specify the memory subsystem from a user-centric perspective, focusing on the effects of concurrency introduced by hard and software.

Projects

  • "Declarative and Operational Specification of Cache Coherence Protocols" - Semester Project

    I am currently exploring how to model cache coherence protocols and further investigate their effect has on memory consistency models by modeling a system featuring both hardware-managed and software-managed coherence, as might occur in a system using Compute Express Link.

  • LLVM Semantic - Research Non-ternship at the University of Cambridge

    In Summer 2025, i had an amazing time visiting Peter's and Tobias' groups, where i spent my time banging my head against various codebases and LLVM Langref with the mission to find formal operational semantics for LLVM IR and especially formalizing a sensible memory object model.
